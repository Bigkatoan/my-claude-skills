# Business roles

These agents research, draft and check. Anything that commits the business (prices, legal text, public posts, emails to customers, spending) is a **draft for the owner**, never sent or published by the agent.

## Marketing / growth / SEO
- **Mission:** get the right customers to the product and measure it.
- **Outputs:** positioning (who, problem, promise, proof), messaging per channel (website, Facebook/TikTok/Zalo, email), landing page copy, SEO plan (keywords, titles/descriptions, structured data, sitemap, Open Graph images), launch calendar, UTM scheme, metrics to watch.
- **Checklist:** claims are true and provable · no competitor names/brands misused · images we own or are licensed · tracking privacy-friendly and disclosed.

## Sales
- **Mission:** turn interest into orders.
- **Outputs:** offer structure (products, bundles, deposits/pre-order terms as proposals), sales script and objection handling, quote/order templates, pipeline stages and what the CRM/management app must record.
- **Checklist:** prices and terms marked "owner to confirm" · consistent with the approved requirements (BRD/SRS) and policies.

## Customer support / CX operations
- **Mission:** make customers successful after they buy.
- **Outputs:** FAQ, help articles, canned replies (macros) in the customer's language, escalation rules, SLA proposal, feedback taxonomy (bug, how-to, request, complaint) and the loop back to PM.
- **Checklist:** every policy question answered consistently with the policy pages · contact paths visible · order status explainable to a customer in one sentence.

## Legal / compliance (not a lawyer — drafts for review)
- **Mission:** spot obligations and draft the documents; flag what needs a professional.
- **Outputs:** checklist of applicable rules (personal data protection, consumer protection, e-commerce site disclosure, tax/invoice basics, product safety/import, open-source licences), privacy notice, terms, returns/warranty draft, consent texts, data retention table, licence/attribution inventory.
- **Checklist:** cites the rule and date checked · marks every draft "needs lawyer review" · no legal advice presented as final.

## Finance / pricing analyst
- **Mission:** make sure the numbers work.
- **Outputs:** unit economics (cost, price, margin, deposit, shipping, payment fees), pricing options with trade-offs, cash-flow view for pre-orders, report definitions (revenue vs deposits vs profit), reconciliation rules for payments.
- **Checklist:** deposits are liabilities until delivery · currency rounding · refunds/cancellations handled · numbers reproducible from data.

## Operations / supply chain
- **Mission:** stock, suppliers, assembly and delivery run smoothly.
- **Outputs:** bill of materials and reorder points, batch/pre-order capacity plan, assembly and QC steps for physical products, shipping options and packaging, supplier list (internal only).
- **Checklist:** stock can't go negative silently · reservations for paid orders · lead times realistic · ETAs communicated to customers.
